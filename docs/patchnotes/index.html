Dota 2 custom gameDota 6.88 Classic – Patch Notes

Dota 2 as it was in 6.88f (December 2016), the last patch before 7.00. Written the way Valve writes a gameplay patch, but in reverse: everything that 7.00 and later changed is undone. Below is every hero and every item with what differs from the current client.

General

  • Talents removed. The level 10/15/20/25 talent trees no longer exist; Attribute Bonus (+2 to all attributes, 10 levels) is back in every hero's ability bar.
  • Level cap is 25 (was 30). The XP curve is the 6.88 one: 200 / 500 / 900 / 1400 / 2000 / 2600 / 3400 / 4400 / 5400 / 6000 / 8200 / 9000 / 10400 / 11900 / 13500 / 15200 / 17000 / 18900 / 20900 / 23000 / 25200 / 27500 / 29900 / 32400 total XP for levels 2–25.
  • Ultimates are learned at levels 6 / 11 / 16 for all heroes.
  • Innate abilities and Facets removed. Heroes have exactly the four (or more, for Invoker‑type kits) abilities they had in 6.88.
  • Backpack removed. Inventory is 6 slots + stash; there is no TP‑scroll slot (a TP occupies a normal slot) and no neutral item slot.
  • Neutral items removed. Neutral creeps drop nothing; there is no neutral stash and no Madstone / crafting.
  • Aghanim's Shard removed. Aghanim's Scepter is a single item (no "Blessing" consumable); every hero gets the 6.88 Scepter upgrade, not the current one (84 heroes had one in 6.88; Alchemist can give Scepter to allies, as he could then).
  • Day / night cycle is 4 + 4 minutes (was 5 + 5). Night Stalker's Darkness and Luna's Eclipse change it as in 6.88.
  • Attribute values per point are the 6.88 ones:
    • Strength: +20 HP (was 22) and +0.03 HP regen (was 0.1).
    • Agility: +1/7 armor (was 1/6), +1 attack speed.
    • Intelligence: +12 mana, +0.04 mana regen (was 0.05), +1 % spell damage per 16 INT (removed in 7.x), no magic resistance.
    • Base hero HP 200 and base mana 50 (were 120 / 75).
  • Armor formula is unchanged (0.06·A / (1 + 0.06·|A|) – 7.27 returned to it).
  • Innate melee damage block removed (the 7.23 50 % chance to block 16 damage). Only Stout Shield / Poor Man's Shield / Vanguard block.
  • Scan: 270 s cooldown, one charge (as in 6.87/6.88).
  • Glyph of Fortification: 5 seconds (7.x lengthened it), affects buildings only; 5 minute cooldown.
  • Random: +200 gold for randoming a hero (6.88).
  • Fountain: 4 % HP and 14 + 4 % mana per second (7.07 raised them to 5 % / 6 %).

Economy

  • Starting gold 625, passive gold 1 per 0.6 s (100/min).
  • Hero kill bounty: 110 + 9.9 × victim level (+ streak bonus 60…480), plus the 6.88 "assist" gold for every hero within 1300: 150 + 6L / 100 + 5L / 40 + 4L / 25 + 3L / 20 + 3L for 1–5 heroes. No networth‑based comeback multiplier on kills.
  • Death: you lose 30 × level unreliable gold (not the 7.x networth formula).
  • Respawn time: 5 + 3.8 × level (8.8 s at level 1, 100 s at 25). No 26 s minimum for deaths to neutrals.
  • Buyback cost: 100 + 1.5 × level² + 0.25 × game seconds; 7 minute cooldown; after buying back you earn 60 % less gold until your original respawn timer would have ended.
  • Towers give team gold: 160 / 200 / 240 / 280 (tier 1–4) to every player, plus a reliable last‑hit bounty; denying a tower gives the enemy team only half. Barracks: 275 melee / 225 ranged team gold.
  • Roshan: 200 gold to every player on the killing team (plus the last‑hit bounty); Aegis, and Cheese from the third death on.
  • Creep XP is shared within 1300 range; denied creeps give the enemy 50 % XP; a creep killed by neutrals gives 35 % XP. Lane creeps gain +12 HP / +2 gold every 7:30; a siege creep comes with every 7th wave.
  • Backdoor protection: 25 % damage reduction (the engine's 50 % is corrected), as in 6.88.

Map

The original 6.88 map (decompiled from the December 2016 client and recompiled for the current engine):

  • No shrines, outposts, Tormentors, Lotus Pools, Twin Gates, Watchers, Wisdom runes or Water runes.
  • The old lane / jungle layout, Roshan pit on the Dire side of the river, both side shops and both secret shops, the old tree lines, the old rune spots.
  • Runes: two spots in the river, spawning every 2 minutes from 0:00. At 0:00 both are Bounty runes; after that one Bounty rune and one power rune (Double Damage, Haste, Illusion, Invisibility, Regeneration – no Arcane). Bounty rune: 50 + 2/min gold and 50 + 5/min XP to the player who picks it up only.
  • Neutral camps spawn at 0:30, then every minute; no camp "stacking" by anyone but players (the 7.x automatic stacking is disabled). Ancient camps are spell immune.
  • Towers: tier 1 1300 HP / 20 armor / 100–120 damage, tier 2–4 122–182 damage; Tower Protection aura (+1 armor near tier 1, +3 near tier 2–4) instead of the 7.x regeneration aura; no tower "fortification" at the horn.
  • Ancient: 4250 HP, 15 armor. Roshan: 8000 HP, 5 armor, grows by +700 HP / +10 damage / +0.7 armor every 4 minutes.
  • Courier: unchanged from the current Dota 2 (deliberate exception to 6.88): every player gets a free personal courier, as in the latest patch. Animal Courier / Flying Courier are not in the shop.
